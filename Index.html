ABOUT — OPERATION IRON SHADOW

You are Captain Alex Carter, special operations. The mysterious IRON DIRECTORATE has seized strategic locations across a fictional region of the Korean Peninsula. Your campaign: infiltrate the border checkpoint, extract the informant MAGPIE from Sokchon Village, recover classified documents from Kyeongam Base, expose the Directorate's plans in their underground research facility, and end it all at the summit fortress — face to face with Colonel Dragan Voss.

A complete single-player FPS: 5 missions, 6 weapons, enemy AI with patrol/cover/suppression, health regen, night-vision goggles, aim assist, instant mission restarts, boss fight, cinematics, full PC + mobile controls, procedural audio and scalable graphics.

Version 1.0 · All characters, locations, units and political organizations are fictional. No real countries, armies or events are depicted.
